# Privacy policy: Research Lanes

Last updated November 5, 2026

What it accesses: files in its owner's Google Drive. It lists one Intake folder, reads the research prompts saved there, and creates one text file per answer in an Output folder. It never moves, renames or deletes files.

How it's used: only to answer the research prompts its owner saved, and to save those answers back to the owner's own Drive.

Where it's kept: on the owner's own home server and in the owner's own Google Drive. Nothing is sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access: the account owner can revoke it at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
